# Order Tracking

## How Do I Track My Order?

Lapalfa uses Shiprocket for shipment tracking.

After your order is shipped, you will receive an email from Shiprocket within 24 to 48 hours.

The email contains:

- Tracking number
- Courier company details
- Expected delivery date

## Track Your Shipment

You can track your shipment here:

https://lapalfa.shiprocket.co/tracking

The tracking page provides two search options:

- Order ID
- AWB

Enter the relevant Order ID or AWB on the tracking page and submit it to check the shipment status.
