# Frequently Asked Questions

## What Shipping Methods Are Available?

We dispatches all parcels using a predefined courier according to your order and location to offer you a fast and reliable delivery service.

Courier partners:

- Delhivery
- FedEx
- Ecom Express
- Blue Dart
- Aramex
- Gati
- Dotzot
- Ekart Logistics
- Xpressbees
- DHL
- Rapid Logistic
- Shadowfax

## How Long Will It Take To Get My Package?

Your parcel will be delivered within 5-8 working days after dispatched from our warehouse.

## How Do I Track My Order?

You will get an email from **Shiprocket** regarding the shipment of order within 24 to 48 hours after shipping.

It contains:

- Tracking number
- Courier company's website details
- Expected date of delivery

After receiving your tracking ID, you can check your parcel status at:

https://lapalfa.shiprocket.co/tracking

## Do I Need An Account To Place An Order?

No, it is not necessary.

However, if you have an account on our website, you can easily find:

- All your order details
- Tracking ID
- Other order information

Having an account can also be helpful for future purchases.

## How Much Does Shipping Cost?

We offer free shipping on orders totaling a minimum of **₹599**.

Please note that we ship only within India.

For orders totaling less than **₹599**, there is a delivery charge of **₹60 to ₹150**.
