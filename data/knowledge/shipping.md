Product-Specific Policies
1. Laptop Battery – Replacement, Exchange & Refund Policy
Replacement / Exchange Rules
Eligible if the battery exhibits any of the following faults during the warranty period:

Backup less than 30 minutes

Not charging

Percentage not increasing

Fails to hold charge

Any other functional fault affecting normal laptop use

Warranty period: Internal → 6 months; External → 1 year

Process:
Contact us via email (preferred) or WhatsApp and provide photos/videos showing the battery issue.

After verification, we will provide the warehouse address.

Customer must pack the battery securely and send it via Indian Speed Post.

Shipping costs are borne by the customer.

Replacement will be sent after warehouse inspection, processing time 7–10 days.

Exclusions:
Batteries that are burnt, swollen, scratched, broken, opened, or physically damaged are not eligible.

Until the battery reaches the warehouse and is inspected, no replacement or exchange will be processed.
